# Axis Bank — Business Analysis

A teardown of Axis Bank Ltd (NSE: AXISBANK) from its filings, earnings-call transcripts, shareholder Q&A, and financial press. Goal: identify the **core** of the business, how it's **leveraged for growth**, and the **bottlenecks**.

**Window:** Q2 FY25 (Sep-2024) → Q1 FY27 (Jun-2026) · **As of:** 2026-08-20 · Standalone, ₹ crore.

## The thesis in one line

Axis runs a **healthy, conservatively-underwritten deposit-and-fee engine**, but **FY26 profit fell ~7% while loans grew 19%** — because the binding constraint has **migrated from deposits to margin**: a structurally thin CASA base (~38%) drove NIM to a cycle-low 3.46%, and three straight quarters of lumpy provisioning added an earnings-consistency "credibility tax." The ~30% valuation discount to ICICI/HDFC is **earned, not anomalous**, until NIM recovers toward the 3.8% aspiration.

## Method & caveats

Three parallel research streams (financial core / business model / bottlenecks) against Axis quarterly press releases, Q4 FY26 & Q1 FY27 earnings-call transcripts, the FY25 annual report, RBI-related disclosures, and financial press (Moneycontrol, Business Standard, ET, Outlook Business, IIFL, Nuvama). Where media figures disagreed with filings, the filing was used and the discrepancy flagged. **Peer comparisons are approximate/secondary** — validate before formal use. Reconstruction for study; **not investment advice.**
