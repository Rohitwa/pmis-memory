The Rules

Personal — a who · natural / artificial / representative
Debit the receiver, credit the giver.

People, firms, banks, clubs — and stand-ins like Outstanding Salary or Prepaid Rent, which hide a person behind a timing word.

Real — a what · tangible / intangible
Debit what comes in, credit what goes out.

Property the firm owns: cash, furniture, machinery, stock — plus goodwill, patents, trademarks.

Nominal — a why · expense-loss / income-gain
Debit all expenses & losses, credit all incomes & gains.

Salary, rent, wages, losses on one side; sales, commission received, discounts received on the other.

The method, always: split the transaction into its accounts → classify each account → apply that account’s own rule → check left total = right total.
