Naming convention
<YYYY-MM-DD>_<Type>_<Party>_<Reference>[_<Amount>][_<Paid by>].<ext>

Type    PO · Proforma · TaxInvoice · Receipt · Expense · Payout
Date    the document's own date, never the upload date
Amount  rupees, no separators — 5344, not 5,344.00

Three of the six expense receipts were all named Expenses-Rohit_2026-07-09_Rohit.jpg, so the only way to tell them apart was to open each one and read the amount. They are now named for their vendor and value. The superseded first version of the Pre-PoC tax invoice was pulled out of the working folder rather than deleted.

On sharing

  • The structure is ready to hand to a CA or an auditor as-is, and the README travels with it.
  • I can share the folder with a specific person by email address, at reader, commenter or writer. Say the word and the address.
  • I cannot switch it to anyone with the link — that toggle is not exposed to me. You would set that from Drive's own share dialog on Finance Tracker Bills, and it inherits down to every month and type folder.
