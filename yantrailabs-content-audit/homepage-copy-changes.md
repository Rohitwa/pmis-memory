# yantrailabs.com — Homepage Copy Changes

Follow-up to the presentation audit (CFO audience). Two changes: the hero line, and the merged ask section.

---

## 1. Hero section — new headline

**Current:**

> A $120 million company is saving up to $3.6 million on its outflows — with AiFA.

**New (assertive):**

> **A $120M revenue company saved $3.6 million with AiFA.**

Why this version works harder:

- **Past tense, done deal.** "Saved" is a fact; "is saving up to" is a projection. CFOs discount projections automatically.
- **No hedge.** "Up to" is gone. If $3.6M is real, state it. If it needs a hedge, the number isn't ready for the hero.
- **Shorter.** 10 words instead of 16. It fits on one line at hero type sizes and reads in a single eye-fixation.
- **"Revenue" anchors the ratio.** The reader immediately computes 3% of revenue — which is exactly the leak number the problem section then confirms. The page becomes one continuous proof of "3%."

Keep the existing subheadline unchanged — it's already the strongest line on the page:

> It reads, matches, checks, closes and pays — inside the ERP you already run.

Keep one CTA under it: **Check your savings →**

Optional micro-addition below the CTA (one line, small type), to pre-qualify without a separate section:

> For companies moving $120M+ a year.

## 2. Merged ask section (replaces both "See how much outflow you're leaving on the table" and "The savings check")

One section, one headline, one CTA. The audit-pitch copy and the form now live together, so the reader never sees two competing asks.

### Section headline

> **See how much outflow you're leaving on the table.**

### Subhead

> We'll run 90 days of your highest-spend vendor invoices through the agents and show you what's recoverable — with evidence, not estimates.

### Left column — what happens (3 steps)

1. **One export. No connector.** Send us an extract your team already produces. About a minute of your time.
2. **The agents run your 90 days.** Duplicates, overcharges, credits not claimed, clocks about to breach — each finding with the invoice attached.
3. **Findings within one working day.** A savings estimate calibrated to your spend and your ERP. You watch the agents hold overcharges live.

Qualifier line (kept, moved under the steps):

> If your company moves over $120 million a year, we'll show you where the leak is — on your own data.

### Right column — the form (unchanged fields)

Name · Work email · Company · Your role · ERP · Annual outflow · Anything specific you want checked (optional)

**CTA button:** `Check your savings →`

> Fix: remove the `₹` from the current button ("₹Check your savings→") — the page speaks in dollars throughout; the currency mismatch reads as a typo.

**Confirmation message (unchanged):**

> Got it. We'll come back within one working day with what we need for the 90-day check — usually a single export.

**Fallback line (unchanged):** Sooner? rohit@yantrailabs.com

### What gets deleted

- The standalone "Book a demo" CTA from the old first ask section — every path now funnels to the single "Check your savings →" button. ("Book a demo" can remain in the top nav for people who arrive pre-sold.)
- The duplicated "duplicates, overcharges, credits not claimed, clocks about to breach" list — it appeared twice; it now appears once, in step 2.
