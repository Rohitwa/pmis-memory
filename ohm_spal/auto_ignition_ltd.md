## 3. Fallback rule applied: where sector assumptions substitute for company evidence

Items tagged [S] above carry no company-specific evidence. They are retained because they are structurally true of every Indian Tier-1 auto-electrical supplier and should be confirmed in the first discovery conversation:

1. OEM annual price-down terms (P1) — assumed 1–3%/yr.
2. Material cost ratio (C1) — assumed 60–68% of revenue.
3. Genset segment position in large-kVA growth (V3) — assumed weak given product range.
4. Export realisation premium and FX (P4) — assumed present, share unknown.
5. Depreciation step-up (O4) — assumed from capex timing.

Two unverified company facts that materially change the picture and should be confirmed early:
- Status of the magnetless axial-flux motor (C2): if real and in production, it is both a cost hedge and a differentiated export product.
- Seller and scale of the acquired alternator/wiper division: it drove FY25 growth; its integration state affects C3, C4 and W2.
