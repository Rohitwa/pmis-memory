The problem

Your ERP sees everything. Someone still has to look.

The ERP records what happens. People turn those records into the tasks the business runs on. The gaps are in that turning.

What the ERP does

Data comes in from the floor, the gate, the store and the bank. Data goes out as ledgers, stock lists and reports. People process it to run six tasks.

One task, two ways

A truck of raw material arrives at the gate. Follow it to the decision it should trigger. Left is how it runs today. Right is the same task with OHM reading the ERP's data as it comes out.

At Wakefit

The gaps have names here.

Wakefit runs SAP across five plants, seven holding points and 165 stores. Each gap is written into its own filings.

The recording gap

3 years

The stock figure given to the bank did not match the books in FY22, FY23 and FY24.

  • Inventory rose from ₹164 crore to ₹262 crore in six months.March to September 2025, RHP
  • 104 days of inventory against a sector norm of 80 to 90. That is ₹50 to 80 crore of stock the business does not need to hold.DRHP, derived
  • Stock is the bank's security and the biggest operating problem. Nobody has one number for it.Auditors' CARO remarks, DRHP

The attention gap

None

No internal audit function until listing. Accounting software with no record of who changed what.

  • 14% of stock is provided against as slow-moving. It was 25% in FY23, a charge of about ₹36 crore in one year.DRHP inventory note
  • A store employee fabricated bills. Small in money, but it is the one control sample disclosed, with 80 new stores a year coming.Patna FIR, January 2025
  • Thousands of bills a month across plants and stores, and four or five checks per bill is the ceiling for any person.

The decision gap

30%

Expected inflation on mattress material cost once old stock runs out.

  • Polyol and TDI rose 30 to 160% in March 2026. 38% of raw material is imported, with no long-term contracts.FY26 results, DRHP
  • The only hedge is pre-buying. Raw material stock doubled from ₹61 to ₹105 crore in nine months.DRHP
  • Management says the margin is borrowed from old inventory. A daily price is answered with a quarterly buy, decided on a monthly picture.FY26 commentary

₹24 crore

A year, added to the bottom line when the three gaps close. Range ₹16 to 34 crore.

₹3 to 7 crfewer write-downs on stock that aged unseen
₹4 to 9 crcarrying cost on ₹50 to 80 crore less stock

A stack that adds about ₹24 crore a year to Wakefit's bottom line by carrying less stock, timing foam-chemical buys to the market, and making OHM the one number that the plants, the books and the bank all trust.
