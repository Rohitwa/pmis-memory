### "While they are still running"

| Evidence | Source |

Today each leak is found at month-end, closure or audit. The claim is that the check happens on the day the bill arrives, with the live project margin beside it.

### "Checking every vendor bill against the BoQ, the PO and the tax rules"

| Check | What it catches | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| Bill vs PO vs BoQ line | Overrun, over-billing, rate above the best rate paid elsewhere | SBU P&L accuracy; project margin |
| Bill vs client scope | Vendor work not in the client's contract: an unbilled change order | Recovers revenue, not just cost |
| GSTR-2B and vendor filing status | Credit that must be reversed with 18% interest; DRC-01C notices | Many small vendors, many filing errors |
| Udyam status and the 45-day clock | Section 43B(h) disallowance; late-payment interest at 3x bank rate | Most site contractors are micro or small enterprises |
| Advances and site cash | Advances left unadjusted; cash above ₹10,000 a day (Section 40A(3)) | Each site runs an imprest |
| Closure checklist | Retention, final client bill, final vendor settlement left open after handover | Cash locked after the project ends |

VIZWORLD already holds the procurement side digitally, so the PO and BoQ exist to match against. That is what makes real-time checking feasible here and not at a WhatsApp-run competitor.

### What the tagline does not claim

OHM does not win projects, price them, or design them. It does not change retention terms, client payment behaviour or the cost of labour. It does not fund the working-capital gap. The claim is confined to what leaks between the quote and the payment, and to making the project margin visible while there is still time to act on it.

## 5. Step 4: OHM controllability filter

OHM scope for this account, per the onboarding note: it sits at the centre of every bill, payment and client invoice, runs 22 checks on each voucher the moment it arrives, holds or flags anything that leaks, and shows live project margin. Flipspaces' team decides. ERP stays the system of record.

| Rank | Lever | OHM effect | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C1/C2 Vendor bill leakage and BoQ overrun | **Controls** | Bill vs PO vs BoQ vs best rate paid; duplicates; holds with evidence attached |
| 2 | P1 Unbilled change orders | **Controls** | Vendor work outside client scope raised as a change order; drafts the client variation bill |
| 3 | T1/T2 MSME and GST | **Controls** | Udyam check and 45-day clock on every voucher; GSTR-2B match; holds payment until the vendor files |
| 4 | W1–W3 Retention, closure, cash | **Influences** | Tracks retention, milestones and receivables by project; closure checklist with due dates. Does not change contract terms or fund the gap |
| 5 | V2 Expansion | **Influences** | Multi-entity, multi-currency books and bank reconciliation on one platform; integrates acquired books |
| 6 | C5 Procurement price variance | **Influences** | Shows the best rate paid for the same item across regions at bill time |
| 7 | C4 Site cash | **Controls** | Reads site bills and receipts, flags spend above the limit |
| 8 | W5 Audit trail | **Controls** | Every voucher carries its evidence and log |

Net: OHM controls five of the eight levers directly. This is the best fit of the four accounts, because the product's core job and the company's named gap are the same sentence.

### Objections to prepare for

- "VIZWORLD already does procurement." Answer: it holds the PO side. The leak is between the PO and the payment, and in bills that never touch VIZWORLD.
- "Our SBU heads watch their numbers." Answer: at month-end, by unit. A loss on one project is invisible inside a unit's month until closure. The posting for a Sr VP Ops says the same.
- "We are growing too fast to add process." Answer: nothing moves without a person's approval; the ERP does not change; the audit is free and read-only.
