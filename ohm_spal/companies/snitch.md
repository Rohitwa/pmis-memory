## 0. Identity check

Snitch Apparels Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru (CIN U18109KA2022PTC163969). Founder Siddharth Dungarwal, who ran a garment buying house for Arvind, Madura and V-Mart from 2012 to 2019, launched Snitch as B2B in late 2019 and went D2C in July 2020 with 35 SKUs. Shark Tank India all-shark deal in 2023. Private; Series B of about $40M led by 360 ONE Asset in June 2025 at a ₹2,500 cr valuation; total raised about $53M; not raising now; IPO target FY30.

Read-through: revenue doubled in FY25 and nearly doubled again in FY26, and the company has stayed within a point or two of break-even throughout. That is the whole story. The founder's claim of "profitable since inception" and the RoC's −1% EBITDA are both true to within rounding. At ₹900 cr with a 2–3% margin, a one-point swing in procurement, marketing or inventory write-down is the difference between profit and loss. Current assets of ₹226 cr against ₹498 cr of revenue say that inventory and receivables already hold about 45% of a year's sales, in a fast-fashion model that is supposed to carry almost no dead stock.

A note on the two FY25 numbers: the RoC filing shows revenue ₹498 cr and a ₹1.7 cr loss; the founder told Inc42 ₹520 cr and ₹30 cr EBITDA, unaudited. The page uses the filing.

## 2. Sector baseline: structural P&L levers for an omnichannel fast-fashion brand in India

Scope: a ₹500–1,000 cr men's fashion brand with small-batch sourcing from garment hubs, 5,000+ SKUs, a store estate growing 70–100% a year through franchise and own stores, marketplaces as the largest online channel, and quick commerce fulfilled from stores. Benchmarks from The Souled Store (₹492 cr FY25), Bewakoof, Rare Rabbit and Zudio.
