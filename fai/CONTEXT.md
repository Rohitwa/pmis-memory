## What FAi is

FAi is a new way to manage finance controls. AI agents check every
transaction — money out, money in, and the entries that record them — against
the company's own rules (approval limits, contracted rates, tax logic,
payment terms), the moment the transaction happens, instead of at month-end.

Strategy (internal, never said to customers): enter through findings — money
the company already lost, proven on their own records — not a platform pitch.
The category is continuous, preventive controls; incumbents are detective.
First engagement: one flow, one entity, findings priced from their own data.

## The agents (15)

"Fifteen agents. Each one a specialist in one mistake."

- **Money out:** Duplicate, Pricing, Payments, Discount
- **Tax:** GST, TDS, PAN, MSME
- **Money in:** Billing, Collections, Cash, Credit
- **The books:** Reconciliation, Intercompany, Journal
- **Evidence Agent** (special billing, always last): backs every catch with
  its source documents; the team confirms or rejects; nothing claimed
  without proof.

Spotlight catches used in UI (illustrative): Duplicate (two invoices, same
supplier/amount, ₹4.2 L), GST (vendor GSTR-3B not filed, credit held ₹1.1 L),
Billing (dispatch with no invoice, ₹7.8 L), Payments (bank account changed by
email, ₹12.6 L held).

## Product metrics (Speed · Accuracy · Savings)

Product-level numbers, never the case study:

- **Speed:** <1 second — every transaction checked the moment it is entered
- **Accuracy:** 90%+ findings confirmed by finance teams, each backed by
  source documents
- **Savings:** 3% of revenue recoverable across payments, tax, pricing and
  collections

(90%+ and 3% are placeholders shaped for real engagement values.)

## Language rules (strict, external material)

1. State, don't perform — no rhetorical questions, no lines for rhythm.
2. Every number is sourced or labelled illustrative. Nothing else in writing.
3. Nouns and amounts, not adjectives.
- Never write: continuous controls monitoring, control latency, preventive/
  detective, shadow mode, straight-through processing, AI-powered,
  transform/unlock/empower, wedge/land-and-expand (internal only).
- The case study anchors the hero and video only; sections carry their own
  content without repeating it.

## What FAi does not do (sales/proposal material, not on the site)

1. Does not post to the ledger — flags and holds; approvers unchanged.
2. Does not make a company compliant by itself — holds payment/credit until
   the counterparty complies.
3. Does not make accounting judgements — collects their evidence.
4. Does not forecast or plan — raises the data ceiling for those who do.
