Every company runs an ERP, but the ERP doesn't do the work — people do. Typing, checking, reporting, chasing. The routine part goes to the agent; the judgment stays with your team.

06 · Boundaries

What it is not

Worth being clear, because finance teams always ask.

It does not replace your ERP +
SAP, Oracle or Tally stays the master record. The agent writes into it — it never becomes the system of record itself.
It does not file your returns +
It prepares them. Your accountant still reviews and signs.
It does not post without a check +
Maker–checker is enforced on every entry, with limits you set.
It does not remove your finance team +
It removes the typing. Your people move to the 30% of the work that needs a brain.

Your books train your deployment, and only yours. Nothing is shared across customers.
